The Social, Profiles, Safety, and Voice APIs enable you to create an enjoyable player community around your game. Profiles are the foundation that social and voice build from with the safety APIs as the gate to enable text communications and voice chat.

## Profiles APIs

Every player is identified by `ngp_profile` that contains the `player_id` (the internal, stable identifier) and `handle` (a player's public-facing display name for the UI). You access the profile from the Company account (`ngp_account`). You then use `ngp_profile` for social and voice events. You may also need the profile APIs to render a player's name in other places of your UI, such as a leaderboard.

## Social APIs

The Social APIs scope to a single `ngp_player` and how that player relates to others. These APIs include friends, blocking, reporting, and a player's presence.

Presence indicates a player's online status and what they're doing. `ngp_social_presence` tells you when a player is online, in what game, and can be used to broadcast this information to other players (if they are friends and enabled by the player) to see.

Friends APIs enable relationships between your players and helps maintain a positive play experience (blocking or reporting a player when needed). A series of events fire when players add, remove, or block other players through the Company UI (`Company_modal.h`).

The Voice APIs enable voice chat functionality in your game, and these APIs are scoped to the C SDK, not your players. There is only one active voice channel per SDK instance.

Each player has their own `audio_state` that cycles between:
- `IDLE` - Connected, but not talking.
- `MUTED` - Microphone off.
- `UNMUTED` - Microphone on, but not actively speaking.
- `SPEAKING` - Actively speaking/making sound. This is where you visually indicate a player is speaking in your UI.

## Best practices for the social experience APIs

- **Always moderate text before display.** Even handles.
- **Pick the right safety rating for your game's audience.** This is becoming increasingly important with different regional legislation regarding the download and playability of applications and games.
- **Don't build your own "add friend" UI.** Open Company's modal instead.
- **Treat presence as advisory, not authoritative.** "Online" might lag reality by seconds. Don't gate game logic on it.
- **For voice, copy data out of callbacks immediately.** The pointers are valid for the callback's lifetime only.
    - **One voice channel at a time.** If you need to switch, call `leave_channel` first, wait for the state callback to confirm, then `join_channel` on the new one.
- **Show audio-state visually.** Players really want to know who's talking — light up the avatar, badge the icon, etc.
